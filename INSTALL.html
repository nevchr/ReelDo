ReelDo.

Make your saved reels
into something you do.

Your extension is already built. No terminal or developer software needed.

Already installed? Add TikTok favourites

  1. Open chrome://extensions and find ReelDo. Click its Reload button to apply version 0.5.0.
  2. Refresh your open Instagram Saved collection and your ReelDo workspace.
  3. Scan again with a limit of 100 to add thumbnails to your earlier saves. Select Refresh thumbnails on existing saves if older images stopped loading. New captures can be organized with Sort my reels.

Version 0.3.2 adds the midnight + mint logo, browser-toolbar icon, and matching workspace accents. It also saves small local copies of visible thumbnails and retries missing images even when captions are present. Keep Instagram selected while the scan scrolls through your grid. The scan reports how many local thumbnails were saved. Preferences → Dark mode changes and saves your theme immediately. Your task edits and completion are preserved.

1Add the extension

  1. In Chrome, type chrome://extensions in the address bar. In Edge, type edge://extensions.
  2. Switch on Developer mode, then click Load unpacked.
  3. Select the dist folder inside this project. If you are using the ZIP, extract it first and select the extracted folder containing manifest.json.
  4. Use the puzzle-piece Extensions menu to pin ReelDo to the toolbar.

Sync TikTok favourites from your browser

  1. Open your TikTok profile and its Favourites tab.
  2. Click ReelDo in the browser toolbar, then Sync TikTok favourites. Keep automatic local sorting enabled to fill your lists.
  3. Keep the page selected. Use the page panel to stop; missing captions remain in Needs context.

The scanner is fixture-tested; your live TikTok layout and account remain unverified. If ReelDo cannot identify the selected profile tab, it asks you to confirm the Favourites view. It stops at sign-in and verification challenges.

Import TikTok favourites as a fallback

  1. Request a JSON data export in TikTok, download it and extract the ZIP.
  2. In ReelDo, choose Import reels, then Choose a file. Select the JSON export or its Favorite Videos.txt.
  3. Review the counts and choose Import & review. Only named favourites sections are read from JSON; text files must contain favourite videos only.
  4. Choose Sort my reels. Entries without captions go to Needs context; paste the actual caption or transcript there to organize them.

This imports local files and does not sync your TikTok account. Existing saves and tasks are preserved. Short share links must be replaced with full video links. Files are limited to 10 MB and 2,000 entries.

2Scan your saves

  1. Open Instagram and log in normally.
  2. Open your profile → Saved → a collection.
  3. Click ReelDo in the browser toolbar, choose a scan limit, and click Sync Instagram saves.
  4. Keep the tab open. The progress panel lets you stop the scan at any time.

3Keep the good ideas

  1. Click Open my workspace in the ReelDo popup.
  2. Choose Sort my reels. Useful ideas automatically go into topic lists, movies into Movies, and memes into Filtered out.
  3. Hover over an entry to modify it in Saved reels or open its original Instagram or TikTok video. These actions stay visible on touch screens.
  4. Use checklists, stars, and dates to put the idea into action. Export a backup to keep a copy.

What this version reads: thumbnail captions, thumbnail images or supplied poster frames, and transcripts you paste. Local thumbnail copies work offline. Link-only fallbacks can still expire; rescan with the refresh option while Instagram stays selected. It does not analyze video audio or frames. If a caption is missing, its link stays in Needs context. Scans capture up to 100 links each. Local AI through Ollama is optional; the built-in rules are ready to use.

Data stays on this device. See README.md for verification details and optional local AI setup.
